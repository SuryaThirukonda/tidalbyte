# Third-party material in the exported web assets

Copied from the Unity project's `THIRD_PARTY.md` (repository root), limited to what is in `public/models`,
`public/textures`, `public/fonts` and `public/audio`. Everything here may be redistributed. Nothing was left out for
licence reasons. The one item with conditions is the recorded voice (last section).

Licence texts: `licences/` in this folder (model packs) and `/fonts/` (beside each font).

## Audio (`/audio/`)

- `sfx/*.wav`: made by this project's own code (`Assets/WishWorld/Scripts/Core/Sfx.cs`). This project's own work.
- `voice/*.mp3` (354 lines): generated with **ElevenLabs** text-to-speech (elevenlabs.io), stock voice "Jessica", model
  `eleven_flash_v2_5`, on a free-plan account. According to the Unity project's notes the free plan asks
  for this credit and is for **non-commercial use**. That fits a hackathon demo; check ElevenLabs' current terms before
  any wider or commercial use, and show the credit ("Voice by ElevenLabs") where the app credits its sources. To do
  without them, delete `/audio/voice` and the `voice` list in `audio.json`; the web app then speaks through `/api/tts`.
