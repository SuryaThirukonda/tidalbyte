# Third-party assets in WishWorld

Everything listed here is free and licensed for this use. Nothing was bought.
The licence texts are in `Assets/ThirdParty/Licences/`.

To build without any prepared assets, set `WISHWORLD_MODELS=generated` in `.env` and run **WishWorld > Rebuild All**: every pack model
is replaced by one generated by this project's own code, the sky photographs give way to painted skies, and the
surroundings keep their terrain and hand-built pieces but lose everything that came from a kit. The fonts are switched off separately
(see "Switching the fonts off" below).

## The team's own toy ("scan a toy")

Bubby, the toy that appears at the end of "scan a toy", is not third-party. The team photographed its own soft toy and
modelled it by hand from those photographs as a generated low-poly mesh (`CreatureMeshes.Bubby()`, built by
`Assets/WishWorld/Editor/Builder.Toy.cs`). No photograph, texture or scan data is in the app, and the toy maker's
ribbon and tag are not modelled. The scan in the app is a staged animation: the headset captures nothing.
It is still a prepared asset. To build without it, set `WISHWORLD_TOY=off` in `.env` and run **WishWorld > Rebuild All**: the toy, its menu button and the
"scan a toy" command are all removed.

turns those into the project's normal prefabs. The original downloads are kept out of the repository

The surroundings (terrain, props to the horizon) are built by `Assets/WishWorld/Editor/Builder.Scenery*.cs`. For those,
`python3 Tools/bake_models.py --kits` bakes every model of the nine kits above into
`Assets/ThirdParty/Baked/Scenery~/<kit>/` (a folder Unity does not import; 944 small files, 13 MB), and the builder
merges the ones it uses into a few static meshes per world. Some are recoloured on the way in: the Nature kit's toy
palette (teal leaves, orange wood, pale blue stone) is mapped to natural greens, browns and greys, and the leaves in the
Pirate, Fantasy Town, Holiday and Survival kits are shifted from blue-green to green. Where no kit had a piece, it was built by hand in
`Assets/WishWorld/Editor/SceneryProps.cs` (barn and silo, houses, chalets, church, acacia, baobab, termite mounds,
igloo, icebergs, ice floes, corals, kelp, anemones, starfish, clams, anchor, rock arches, dinosaur skeleton, nest,
clouds, airship, ski lift, snowmobile, tyre stacks, the grandstand crowd).

## Sky photographs

Every outdoor world, the hub and the race circuit have a real 360 degree sky photograph behind them.
`python3 Tools/bake_skies.py` resizes each to 4096 x 2048, writes `Assets/ThirdParty/Skies/Sky_<Name>.jpg`, and
measures the sun's position and the horizon colour into `skies.json`, which the builder uses to aim the scene's light
at the sun in the photograph and to match the fog to the horizon.

The font files are in `Assets/ThirdParty/Fonts/`. `Assets/WishWorld/Editor/Builder.Fonts.cs` turns them into
TextMesh Pro font assets with fixed atlases in `Assets/WishWorld/Generated/Fonts/` (menu: **WishWorld > Rebuild Fonts**).
Numbers on the race dashboard and the score panel stay in TextMesh Pro's Liberation Sans for legibility.

### Switching the fonts off

Delete `Assets/WishWorld/Generated/Fonts/` and `Assets/ThirdParty/Fonts/`, then run **WishWorld > Rebuild All**.
With the files missing, every text falls back to the plain font (a warning is logged for each).

## Recorded voice

The guide's 354 preset lines in `Assets/WishWorld/Generated/Voice/` were generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech
(elevenlabs.io), stock voice "Jessica", model `eleven_flash_v2_5`, on a free-plan account. The free plan
asks for this credit and is for non-commercial use, as far as I know: check ElevenLabs' current terms before wider use.
To remove them, delete that folder and `Assets/WishWorld/Generated/Resources/VoiceBank.asset`; the guide then uses the
live service, or its placeholder tone when there is no key.
